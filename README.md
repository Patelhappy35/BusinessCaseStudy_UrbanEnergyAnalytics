## 📌 Project Overview

**Watt Watch** is a Power BI dashboard project created to analyze urban energy consumption across different zones and consumer types.

The project focuses on understanding energy consumption patterns, peak usage, power outages, estimated energy costs, and differences between consumer types.

The analysis is performed using **SQL** and visualized using **Microsoft Power BI**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze total energy consumption across different city zones.
- Compare energy consumption among different consumer types.
- Analyze monthly energy consumption trends.
- Monitor average peak energy usage.
- Analyze total power outage minutes.
- Estimate the total energy cost.
- Provide interactive filtering for better analysis.
- Identify energy consumption hotspots and areas that may require better energy management.

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** `SmartCityEnergy.csv`

The dataset contains smart-city energy consumption information.

### Main Columns

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| MeterID | Unique identifier for each smart meter |
| Zone | City zone |
| ConsumerType | Residential, Commercial, or Industrial |
| Date | Reading date |
| EnergyConsumed_kWh | Total energy consumed in kWh |
| PeakUsage_kWh | Maximum kWh drawn in a single hour |
| OutageMinutes | Power outage duration in minutes |
| MeterStatus | Active or Faulty |
| TariffRate | Price per kWh |

# 🔧 SQL Analysis

SQL was used for data analysis before visualization in Power BI.

The project covers the following analysis:

1. Total and average daily energy consumption by zone.
2. Top 5 highest energy-consuming consumers by type.
3. Monthly consumption trends across zones.
4. Average cost per zone using energy consumption and tariff rate.
5. Meters with the highest number of faults or outages.
6. Zones with lower energy efficiency based on high usage and frequent outages.
7. Peak usage patterns during weekdays and weekends.

The SQL analysis results can be exported in tabular form for dashboarding.

The dashboard contains four important KPI cards:

Displays the total outage duration in minutes.

### 💰 Total Estimated Cost
Displays the estimated total energy cost.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Energy Consumption by Zone

A column chart is used to compare total energy consumption across different zones.

This helps identify which zones consume the most energy.

### 2. Energy Usage by Consumer Type

A bar chart compares energy consumption among:

- Residential
- Commercial
- Industrial

This helps determine which consumer type contributes the most to total energy usage.

### 3. Monthly Consumption Trend

A line chart shows the trend of total energy consumption over time.

This helps identify changes and patterns in energy consumption across months.
