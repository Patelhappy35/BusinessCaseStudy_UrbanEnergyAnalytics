ENERGY FINDINGS

1. Total energy consumption across the smart city is 268,324.66 kWh, showing significant overall electricity usage.

2. North Zone is the highest energy-consuming zone with 54,925.53 kWh, making it an important area for energy monitoring and efficiency improvements.

3. Residential consumers use the highest amount of energy at 100,309.94 kWh, followed by Commercial consumers at 90,629.38 kWh.

4. The highest monthly energy consumption was recorded in September 2025 at 24,397.43 kWh, while the lowest was recorded in February 2025 at 19,746.95 kWh.

5. The average peak energy usage is approximately 26.99 kWh, which can help monitor periods of high electricity demand.

6. West Zone has the highest total outage duration of 18,612 minutes, indicating a need for improved power supply reliability in this zone.

7. Out of 5,000 meter records, 492 meters were marked as Faulty, highlighting the need for regular meter maintenance and monitoring.
